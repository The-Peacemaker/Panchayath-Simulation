അധ്യായം 0 · THE CALL

It started with the folder name

Someone named a folder USELESS-PROJECT and suddenly we were researching TinkerHub's most unserious hackathon like it was a UPSC exam. Deep dive done, we struck gold in the official handbook: judging is 60% creativity, 20% implementation complexity, 20% cross-disciplinary approach. Translation — the IDEA is the whole game. No pitch decks. No "real-world impact". Just one night and whatever wonderfully pointless thing comes to mind.

👨‍💼കുട്ടപ്പൻ, ഫയൽ എഴുത്തുകാരൻ"സാർ, 60% ക്രിയേറ്റിവിറ്റി എന്ന് കേട്ടപ്പോൾ തന്നെ എനിക്ക് മനസ്സിലായി — ഇത് നമ്മുടെ പണിയാണ്."
അധ്യായം 1 · FIVE IDEAS, ONE CHAIR

Brainstorming the uselessness

We drafted five concepts — a chai-locking machine, an Amma chatbot, an over-engineered canteen app, a mystery ARG app, an AI that judges your surroundings. All software-only, all Malayalam-soaked. Then somebody said the magic words: "Panchayat President Simulator" — a game where YOU run a Kerala village into the ground, one sarcastic decision at a time. Fully Malayalam audio and text. Timer ticking. No correct answers, only funny ones. Locked in minutes.

അധ്യായം 2 · FOUNDATION

Scaffold first, comedy second

Vite + React 18 + Tailwind. A tiny useReducer game engine (~150 lines) drives everything: HOME → OATH → DAY ×5 → CONSEQUENCE → ENDING. Stats, timers, endings, a "Wall of Presidents" in localStorage. Committed as 4a969f2 scaffold, then a134065 engine. The skeleton was up in hours — the soul took much longer.

അധ്യായം 3 · THE WRITING ROOM

Six crises, zero mercy

Water mafia. Street-dog unions. Exam-eve blackouts. One hall, two weddings. A collector inspection final boss (plus a festival-fund scandal spare). Every option stuffed with Malayalam cinema references — Lucifer mass entries, Drishyam register frauds, Kilukkam mass weddings, Sandesham-style protests. The house rule for writing: no option may be clearly right, and every option must hurt something. The comedy IS the dilemma.

😅കുട്ടപ്പൻ"സർ, ലൂസിഫർ സ്റ്റൈലിൽ ടാങ്കർ കൊണ്ടുവന്നാൽ ഖജനാവ് കാലിയാകും. പക്ഷേ എൻട്രി ഗംഭീരമായിരിക്കും."
അധ്യായം 4 · THE GREAT STAT SWAP

Killing our darlings (elephant joy + amma approval)

Playtesting hurt: four stats were too much to read, and two of them (elephant happiness, amma approval) never decided anything. So we executed them and crowned a single replacement — പ്രതിപക്ഷം (Opposition), starting at 20, killing you at 100 via no-confidence motion. Manikandan the elephant survived as mascot, but now he joins the protest when opposition crosses 70. Fewer meters, ten times the tension.

അധ്യായം 5 · THE EXPLOIT

The day we caught ourselves winning

Disaster: stupid decisions kept winning. Joke options secretly gave support points, the hero ending ignored opposition entirely, and one honest Day-5 pick erased all sins. So we did the most over-engineered thing possible — wrote a Node script that simulated 8 full playstyles through the real game data. Splurge-everything: dead Day 5, bankrupt. Total silence: dead Day 4. Evil genius run: Commission King at opposition 90. Smart mix: hero by exact margins. Balance v3 shipped with proof, not vibes.

😎കുട്ടപ്പൻ"കണക്ക് പറഞ്ഞു സർ — മിണ്ടാതിരുന്നാൽ മരണം. ഇത് ജനാധിപത്യമല്ല, ഗണിതമാണ്."
അധ്യായം 6 · DECLUTTER + SPRITES

Two Kuttappans walk into a screen…

…and one had to go. The Day screen had twin Kuttappans, cards inside cards, labels shouting over the actual story. We merged everything into ONE briefing card, shrank the timer to a slim pill, and replaced every emoji face with a hand-drawn SVG sprite cast — Kuttappan with five mood faces (cool, worried, panic, shock, X-eyed dead), Manikandan with three states (sleeping with Z's, calm, rampaging), plus a smiling coconut mascot for the header. Zero image dependencies, infinite charm.

അധ്യായം 7 · FINDING THE VOICE

13 clips of pure Malayalam chaos

Then came the recordings — welcome speech, pledge reaction, day transitions, rotating wrong/correct-answer stingers, three ending speeches — all performed with full overacting. We built a slot-map engine (voiceLines.js, 47 slots) where dropping an MP3 into a folder wires it into the game with zero code changes, chained playback (sting → briefing → verdict), a verdict scorer that decides praise vs roast, and TTS fallback so nothing is ever silent. Then ffmpeg told us one clip peaked at -19.9 dB — a whisper. Boosted +18.9 dB, then peak-normalized all 12 clips to the same level. Democracy of decibels.

അധ്യായം 8 · BUG HUNT DIARIES

Three ghosts, three exorcisms

🐘 The elephant that wasn't

Symptom: clicking Take Charge played a sound suspiciously like an elephant trumpet.

Culprit: our synth conch horn (210→285 Hz sweep). Majestic. Pachyderm-adjacent.

Fix: retired the conch from that moment — the recorded welcome speech owns it now.

👻 The phantom sting

Symptom: wrong-answer audio leaked onto fresh option screens nobody had touched yet.

Culprit: Chrome fires onend even for cancelled speech — clicking through mid-roast ghost-triggered the chained sting.

Fix: token-guarded every voice callback; cancelled chains die silently. Class of leak: extinct.

🔇 The silent welcome

Symptom: intro recording never played; robot voice covered for it.

Culprit: absolute /voice/… path 404'd outside domain-root hosting.

Fix: Vite base: './' — relative paths everywhere, verified 200 + audio/mpeg over HTTP.

⚖️ The rigged election

Symptom: every playstyle won. Democracy felt fake.

Culprit: joke options paid support points; hero ending ignored opposition.

Fix: the 8-playstyle simulation above. Math restored faith in the republic.

അധ്യായം 9 · SHIPPING

Five commits and a rebase survival story

Shipped in honest feature batches — scaffold, engine, screens, voices, docs — then survived a real-world scare: the remote had moved (teammate's README edit), push rejected, fetched, rebased cleanly, pushed green. Live on Netlify, README carries the template format, screenshots, mermaid diagrams and the live link. The president's portrait (president.jpg) now defaults into every newspaper wanted-box.

💀കുട്ടപ്പൻ, പുതിയ അഡ്മിനിസ്ട്രേറ്റർ (താത്കാലികം)"കസേര കിട്ടി സർ. പക്ഷേ ഫയൽ 420 ഒരിക്കലും അടയ്ക്കില്ല."
പഠിച്ച പാഠങ്ങൾ · LEARNINGS

What the panchayat taught us

Web Audio API: synthesized a conch, chenda drums, a Mohanam-raga jingle and a sad trombone from raw oscillators — no audio files needed.
SpeechSynthesis quirks: Chrome fires onend on cancelled speech; Malayalam voice availability varies by machine — always build fallbacks.
Balance is math: simulating playstyles through real game data beats gut feeling. Steal this trick for every game jam.
Vite base paths: absolute asset URLs silently die off-domain-root. base: './' is one line of invincibility.
SVG sprites: five moods of a clerk drawn in ~100 lines of SVG beat any emoji — and weigh nothing.
Git under fire: fetch → read the diff → rebase → push. Never force-push a shared hackathon repo.
